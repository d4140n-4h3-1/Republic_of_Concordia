# EDUCATION ACT — ASSESSMENT AND PATHWAYS

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Assessment and Pathways Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to measure learning without crushing it: to use assessment to place each student where they will learn best, subject by subject, and never to close a door permanently.

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

This Act applies to every school covered by the National Education System Act, Section 3.

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Sections IV.1.b(3), IV.1.b(15), and IV.1.b(17).

## Section 4 — National Assessments

### 4.a When Given

There shall be national assessments only at the end of primary education and at the end of secondary education.

### 4.b Use

The end-of-primary assessment shall be used only to place each student at a level for each subject in secondary education under Section 5. It shall not be used to exclude any student from secondary education, or to place a student in a separate school.

Results shall be reported as broad achievement levels, not as scores or ranks.

### 4.c No Rankings

No school shall publish a ranking of students. No government shall publish a ranking of schools by assessment results.

## Section 5 — Subject-Based Banding

### 5.a Levels by Subject

In secondary education, each subject shall be offered at more than one level. Each student shall take each subject at the level that suits them in that subject, so that a student may take mathematics at a higher level and a language at a foundation level, or the reverse.

### 5.b Mixed Classes

Students of all levels shall share the same form class, and shall take non-examined subjects together.

### 5.c Movement Between Levels

Every student's level in every subject shall be reviewed each year, and a student may move up at any time on the recommendation of a teacher or at the request of the student and family.

## Section 6 — Lower-Stakes Testing

No student shall sit more than one major examination in a subject in a school year before the end of secondary education. The first two years of primary education shall have no graded examinations.

## Section 7 — Admission to Post-Secondary

Admission to academic colleges, polytechnics, and technical institutes shall rest on the end-of-secondary assessment, the student's record, and the student's interests and aptitudes, including portfolios and interviews for applied programs. Every student who completes secondary education shall be offered a place on at least one pathway in the National Education System Act, Section 4.d.

## Section 8 — Special Educational Needs

A student with a disability shall receive reasonable accommodation in every assessment and placement, and support to learn in a mainstream school wherever practicable (Article I, Section I.4.a).

## Section 9 — Private Tutoring

No public school teacher shall tutor, for pay, a student whom they teach or assess. Every public school shall offer free extra help to students who need it, so that success does not depend on paid tutoring.

## Section 10 — Appeals

A student or family may appeal a placement or assessment result to an impartial reviewer, with a decision within thirty days.

## Section 11 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution.

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 12 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 13 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on the same date as the National Education System Act.
