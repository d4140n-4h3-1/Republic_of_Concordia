# EDUCATION ACT — CURRICULUM AND LANGUAGES

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Curriculum and Languages Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to set a national curriculum framework that teaches fewer things more deeply, builds mastery in mathematics and science, makes every student fluent in two languages, and prepares every student for informed civic life (Article I, Section I.2.a).

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

This Act applies to every school covered by the National Education System Act, Section 3.

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Sections IV.1.b(3), IV.1.b(15), and IV.1.b(17).

## Section 4 — Curriculum Council

### 4.a Establishment

The **Curriculum Council** is established as an independent office. It consists of nine members serving staggered six-year terms: three practicing teachers, three subject experts, and three members of the public, chosen under the public-drawing procedures of Article III, Section III.3.f. No more than three members may be affiliated with the same political party.

### 4.b Duties

The Council shall set the national core standards and the curriculum framework, review them every six years, and publish them with the evidence on which they rest. No elected official shall direct the Council to include or exclude any content.

## Section 5 — Core Curriculum

Every public school shall teach:

1. **language and literacy** in an official language;
2. **a second language** under Section 7;
3. **mathematics**, taught for mastery under Section 6;
4. **science**, including laboratory and field work;
5. **humanities**, including history, geography, and the arts;
6. **civics**, including this Constitution, the rights it protects, and how to take part in government;
7. **character and citizenship**, including ethics, respect for others, and personal finance; and
8. **physical education and health**.

## Section 6 — Teaching for Mastery

### 6.a Depth Over Breadth

The curriculum shall cover fewer topics in greater depth, so that students master each concept before moving on.

### 6.b Mathematics

Mathematics shall be taught by moving from **concrete** objects, to **pictorial** representations, to **abstract** symbols, and shall emphasize problem solving and understanding over memorization.

### 6.c Inquiry

Science and humanities shall be taught through inquiry, projects, and discussion, as well as direct instruction.

## Section 7 — Bilingual Education

### 7.a Two Languages

Every student shall learn an official language, and a second language.

### 7.b The Second Language

A student's second language shall be their native language, where a qualified teacher or approved remote course is available, or another language the student or the student's family chooses (Article I, Section I.1.f).

### 7.c Language Rights

Public education may be provided in an official language. No student shall be punished for using their native language outside instruction that requires another language (Article I, Section I.1.f).

## Section 8 — Workload and Well-Being

### 8.a Time

No school shall require more than **thirty-five hours** of instruction a week, and the guideline for homework shall be no more than **one hour** a day in primary education and **two hours** a day in secondary education.

### 8.b Rest and Play

Every primary school day shall include unstructured time for play. Every student shall have at least one school day in each term free of tests.

## Section 9 — Freedom of Expression

Nothing in this Act permits a school to punish a student for a view expressed in good faith, or requires a student to profess any belief (Article I, Sections I.1.a and I.1.b). Civics shall teach how government works and what the Constitution says, not which party to support.

## Section 10 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution.

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 11 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 12 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on the same date as the National Education System Act. The Curriculum Council shall publish the first national core standards within two years after enactment.
