# EDUCATION ACT — TEACHERS

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Teaching Profession Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The quality of an education system cannot exceed the quality of its teachers. The purpose of this Act is to recruit teachers from among the most capable graduates, train them well, pay them as professionals, and give them careers worth staying in.

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

This Act applies to every teacher in a school covered by the National Education System Act, Section 3.

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Sections IV.1.b(3), IV.1.b(15), and IV.1.b(17).

## Section 4 — Selection

Teacher candidates shall be selected by open competition on academic achievement, subject knowledge, and an interview and teaching demonstration showing aptitude for teaching. Selection shall not consider protected status (Article I, Section I.4.a).

## Section 5 — Paid Training

### 5.a National Institute of Education

The **National Institute of Education** is established to train every new teacher, through a program of at least one year for graduates and four years for those entering after secondary education.

### 5.b Salary During Training

Every candidate shall be paid a salary while training, and shall pay no tuition.

### 5.c Service Commitment

A candidate shall commit to teach in a public school for **three years** after training. A candidate who leaves early shall repay a share of the training cost in proportion to the time not served, and the repayment shall be waived for hardship.

## Section 6 — Pay

Teachers shall be paid on a national scale that is comparable to that of other professions requiring similar education, such as engineering and accounting. No public teacher shall be paid less than that scale because of the wealth of the school's community.

## Section 7 — Professional Development

Every teacher shall be entitled to **one hundred hours** of paid professional development each year, chosen in part by the teacher.

## Section 8 — Career Tracks

Every teacher may advance on one of three tracks, with equal pay at equal levels:

1. **teaching**, for those who wish to remain in the classroom, rising to master teacher;
2. **leadership**, rising to principal and system leader; and
3. **specialist**, in curriculum, assessment, special education, or counseling.

## Section 9 — Class Size

No primary class shall exceed **thirty** students, and no secondary class shall exceed **thirty-five**. Schools serving many students from families in need shall have smaller classes, under Article I, Section I.4.a's permission for benefits that address need.

## Section 10 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution.

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 11 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 12 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on the same date as the National Education System Act. Section 6 shall be phased in over five years.
