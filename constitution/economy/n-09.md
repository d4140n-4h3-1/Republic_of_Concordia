# ECONOMIC ACT — CENTRAL BANK

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Central Bank Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to establish the independent central bank required by Article IV, Section IV.10.f, to keep prices stable, and to keep monetary policy out of the hands of any government that might use it to win an election or pay its bills.

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Sections IV.1.b(5), IV.10.f, and IV.1.b(17).

## Section 4 — Establishment

The **Central Bank of Concordia** is established as the central bank required by Article IV, Section IV.10.f. It alone conducts the monetary policy of Concordia and issues its currency.

## Section 5 — Objectives

### 5.a Price Stability

The primary objective of the Bank is stable prices, defined as inflation of **two percent** a year, measured by a consumer price index the Bank publishes, on average over time.

### 5.b Other Objectives

Without prejudice to price stability, the Bank shall support high and stable employment and the stability of the financial system.

### 5.c Explaining Departures

Whenever inflation is more than one percentage point above or below the target, the Bank shall publish a letter to Congress explaining why, what it is doing about it, and when it expects inflation to return to the target.

## Section 6 — The Board

### 6.a Members

The Bank is governed by a Board of seven members, appointed under Article IV, Section IV.10.f, each for a single term of fourteen years. Each member shall have recognized expertise in economics, finance, or banking.

### 6.b Chair

The Board shall elect one of its members as Chair, for a term of four years, renewable while the member serves. The Chair speaks for the Bank and reports to Congress.

### 6.c Cause for Removal

**Cause** for removal under Article IV, Section IV.10.f means only incapacity, serious misconduct, a conviction for an offense punishable by imprisonment for more than one year, or the persistent neglect of duty. A disagreement with a decision of the Bank is never cause.

### 6.d Conflicts

No member shall hold a financial interest in any bank or financial firm, or trade in any security other than broad public funds, while serving. For two years after leaving the Board, no member shall be employed by a bank or financial firm that the Bank lends to or oversees.

### 6.e Decisions

The Board decides monetary policy by majority vote, at meetings held at least eight times each year. Each member's vote shall be published with the decision.

## Section 7 — Powers

The Bank may:

1. set interest rates on its own lending and deposits;
2. buy and sell public debt and other high-quality debt in the open market, for the purposes of monetary policy, on the same terms as any other buyer (Article IV, Section IV.10.f);
3. lend to solvent banks against good collateral, including as lender of last resort in a crisis, at a rate above its ordinary rate;
4. operate and oversee the systems by which payments are made between banks; and
5. issue notes and coins.

## Section 8 — Prohibitions

The Bank shall not:

1. lend to, or buy debt directly from, any government in Concordia, or otherwise finance public spending (Article IV, Section IV.10.f);
2. lend to a bank it knows to be insolvent, except under a law that provides for resolving that bank;
3. take instructions from any government, party, or official on any decision; or
4. use its powers to favor any business, industry, region, or political party.

## Section 9 — Accountability

### 9.a Publication

The Bank shall publish each monetary policy decision and its reasons when it is made, and the record of the Board's deliberations within **three weeks** after each meeting.

### 9.b Reports to Congress

The Chair shall report to Congress, in public, at least twice each year, on monetary policy, inflation, and the stability of the financial system.

### 9.c Audit

The Auditor-General shall audit the Bank's accounts and operations each year (Article IV, Section IV.10.g). An audit shall not direct or reverse a monetary policy decision.

### 9.d Profits

The Bank's net profits, after reserves set by the Board within limits set by law, shall be paid to the Treasury each year.

## Section 10 — First Board

For the first Board, lot shall assign the seven members to terms ending two, four, six, eight, ten, twelve, and fourteen years after the Bank is established, so that one term ends every two years. Each first-Board term is that member's single term.

## Section 11 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution.

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 12 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 13 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on its enactment. The first Board shall be appointed within one year after enactment, and the Bank shall take over monetary policy and the issue of currency on a date the Board sets within one year after that.
