# ECONOMIC ACT — FAMILIES

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Family Support Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to let parents combine work and family, and to give every child a strong start, through paid parental leave shared between parents, affordable early childhood care, and support for every child.

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

This Act applies to every parent and child resident in Concordia.

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Sections IV.1.b(2), IV.1.b(3), IV.1.b(6), and IV.1.b(17).

## Section 4 — Parental Leave

### 4.a Length

The parents of each child, by birth or adoption, are entitled together to **480 days** of paid parental leave, which may be taken at any time until the child turns **eight**.

### 4.b Reserved Days

**Ninety days** are reserved for each parent and cannot be transferred to the other. A single parent receives all 480 days.

### 4.c Pay

Parental leave is paid at **eighty percent** of the parent's previous wage, up to a ceiling set by law, for 390 days, and at a flat rate set by law for the remaining 90 days.

### 4.d Protection

No parent shall be dismissed, disadvantaged, or denied their position for taking parental leave.

## Section 5 — Early Childhood Care

### 5.a Right to a Place

Every child from the age of **one** has the right to a place in public or publicly funded early childhood care, within a reasonable distance of home, within four months of a request.

### 5.b Fee Ceiling

Fees shall not exceed **three percent** of household income for the first child, **two percent** for the second, and **one percent** for the third, and no family shall pay fees for a fourth or later child. The two free years from age four under the National Education System Act, Section 4.a, remain free.

### 5.c Quality

Care shall meet standards set by law for staff training, group size, and staff-to-child ratios.

## Section 6 — Child Benefit

Every child under the age of **sixteen** shall receive a monthly child benefit, set by law, paid to a parent or guardian, without regard to income.

## Section 7 — Care for Sick Children

A parent may take paid leave to care for a sick child under the age of twelve, for up to **one hundred twenty days** a year, at the rate in Section 4.c.

## Section 8 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution, and applies equally to every family, whatever the protected status of its members (Article I, Section I.4.a).

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 9 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 10 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on the first day of the next fiscal year following its enactment. Section 5.a shall be fully in force within five years.
