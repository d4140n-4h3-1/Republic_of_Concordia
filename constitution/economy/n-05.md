# ECONOMIC ACT — PENSIONS AND THE NATIONAL WEALTH FUND

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Pensions and National Wealth Fund Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to ensure that no person grows old in poverty, that pensions stay sound as the population ages, and that the wealth of the nation's natural resources is saved for every generation rather than spent by one.

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

This Act applies to every resident of Concordia, and to all revenue the federal government receives from natural resources.

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Sections IV.1.b(2), IV.1.b(3), and IV.1.b(17).

---

**Part One — Pensions**

## Section 4 — Three Pillars

Every person's pension is made up of:

1. a **guaranteed pension**, paid from general taxes, which ensures that every resident who has reached pension age has at least a minimum income set by law, reduced as their other pension income rises;
2. an **earnings-related pension**, built from contributions of **sixteen percent** of earnings, recorded in a personal account that grows with average wages and is paid out as a lifelong pension; and
3. a **funded pension**, built from contributions of **two and one-half percent** of earnings, invested in funds the person chooses, or in a low-cost public default fund.

## Section 5 — Pension Age

The earliest pension age is **sixty-three**. The age shall rise with average life expectancy, under a formula set by law and published at least ten years in advance.

## Section 6 — Automatic Balance

If the earnings-related system's assets and future contributions fall short of its future obligations, the growth of pensions shall be reduced automatically until balance is restored, so that the system remains sound without sudden cuts.

## Section 7 — Credits for Care

Contributions shall be credited, from public funds, for periods of parental leave, care for a sick child, military or civilian service, and study.

---

**Part Two — National Wealth Fund**

## Section 8 — The Fund

### 8.a Establishment

The **National Wealth Fund** is established. Every payment the federal government receives for the extraction or use of natural resources, including oil, gas, minerals, and the radio spectrum, shall be paid into it.

### 8.b Investment

The Fund shall be invested abroad, in a broad portfolio of shares, bonds, and real estate, by an independent manager under guidelines set by law. It shall not be invested to influence any company or market in Concordia.

### 8.c Spending Rule

Each year, no more than **three percent** of the Fund's value may be transferred to the federal budget. The rest shall be saved.

### 8.d Ethical Guidelines

The Fund shall not invest in companies that produce weapons that violate fundamental humanitarian principles, that cause severe environmental damage, or that are complicit in serious violations of human rights. An independent **Council on Ethics** shall recommend exclusions, and every exclusion and its reasons shall be published.

### 8.e Transparency

The Fund shall publish every holding, its returns, and its costs, at least once each year.

## Section 9 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution.

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 10 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 11 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on the first day of the next fiscal year following its enactment. Part One applies in full to persons who begin work after that date, and to others under transition rules set by law over twenty years.
