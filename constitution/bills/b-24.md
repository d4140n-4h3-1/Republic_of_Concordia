# BILL — CIVIL SERVICE

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Civil Service Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to carry out Article V, Section V.4.e: to hire and promote public servants on merit, to keep the number of political appointees small, and to protect civil servants who serve the public impartially from being dismissed for their politics or for refusing to break the law.

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

This Act applies to every position in the federal civil service (Article V, Section V.4.e). A state, territory, or local government may adopt it for its own employees.

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Sections IV.1.b(8) and IV.1.b(17), to carry into execution Article V, Section V.4.e.

## Section 4 — Definitions

*(Civil service and political position are defined in Article V, Section V.4.e.)*

### 4.a Cause

**Cause** for dismissal means:

1. serious misconduct;
2. a conviction for an offense that bears on the duties of the position; or
3. performance that remains unacceptable after the employee has been told in writing what must improve, and given at least **ninety days** and reasonable support to improve it.

## Section 5 — Merit

### 5.a Open Competition

Every vacancy in the civil service shall be advertised publicly, with its duties and requirements, for at least **fourteen days**. Applicants shall be assessed by job-related tests, interviews, or other assessments, scored by criteria published in advance. The appointment shall go to one of the highest-scoring applicants.

### 5.b No Political Tests

No applicant or employee shall be asked about, or judged on, their political opinion, party affiliation, vote, or support for any official (Article V, Section V.4.e).

### 5.c Probation

A new employee serves a probation of no more than **one year**, during which they may be dismissed for unsatisfactory performance with written reasons and a right to review under Section 8.

## Section 6 — Political Positions

### 6.a Number

No more than **one percent** of the positions in any department or agency, and no more than an overall number set by law, shall be political positions.

### 6.b List

Every political position shall be designated by law and listed, with its holder, in a public register. A position not on the list is in the civil service.

### 6.c Conversion

No civil service position shall be made a political position while it is held by a member of the civil service, and no person shall be moved from a political position into the civil service except through open competition under Section 5.a.

## Section 7 — Protections

### 7.a Dismissal

A member of the civil service may be dismissed only for cause, or because their position is abolished by law, after written notice of the reasons and an opportunity to respond (Article V, Section V.4.e).

### 7.b Abolished Positions

A member of the civil service whose position is abolished by law shall have priority for any vacant position for which they are qualified, and shall receive the notice and severance of the Flexicurity Act, Section 4.

### 7.c Unlawful Orders and Disclosures

No member of the civil service shall be dismissed, disciplined, transferred, or otherwise penalized for:

1. refusing to carry out an order that would violate the Constitution or a law;
2. disclosing in good faith evidence of wrongdoing, waste, or danger to Congress, the Auditor-General, a court, an inspector, or the Information Commissioner; or
3. lawful political activity outside working time.

Where an adverse action follows such a refusal or disclosure within one year, the department bears the burden of proving it was not taken for that reason.

### 7.d Neutrality

Members of the civil service shall not use their position, working time, or public resources to support or oppose any candidate or party, and shall not be required to (Article V, Section V.4.e).

## Section 8 — Civil Service Commission

### 8.a Establishment

The **Civil Service Commission** is established as the independent body required by Article V, Section V.4.e. It shall hear appeals against dismissal, discipline, and appointment decisions, audit departments' compliance with this Act, and publish the register under Section 6.b.

### 8.b Members

The Commission consists of five members serving staggered five-year terms, nominated by the President and confirmed by three-fifths of the Senate. No more than two members may be affiliated with the same political party. A member may be removed only for cause, after notice and a hearing.

### 8.c Appeals

An employee may appeal within thirty days. The Commission shall decide within ninety days, after a hearing satisfying Article II, Section II.1.a, and may order reinstatement, back pay, and any other remedy. Its decision is subject to review in court.

## Section 9 — No Evasion

No position shall be reclassified, and no function moved to a contractor or another body, for the purpose of evading this Act (Article V, Section V.4.e). The Commission may set aside any such action.

## Section 10 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution.

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 11 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 12 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on the first day of the next fiscal year following its enactment. Every person who holds a position on that date that is not designated a political position becomes a member of the civil service.
