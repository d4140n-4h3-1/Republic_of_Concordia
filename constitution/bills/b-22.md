# BILL — CIVIL LEGAL AID

## Section 1 — Short Title

This Act may be cited as the **Civil Legal Aid Act**.

## Section 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to carry out Article II, Section II.1.e, so that no person loses a case, their home, their child, or their liberty because they could not pay a court fee or a lawyer.

## Section 3 — Scope and Authority

This Act applies to every civil proceeding in a federal court or before a federal tribunal. Sections 5 and 6 also set the standards that every state and territory shall meet in its own courts under Article II, Section II.1.e.

Congress enacts this Act under Article IV, Section IV.1.b(15), to enforce Article II, Section II.1.e against every level of government, and under IV.1.b(3) and IV.1.b(17).

## Section 4 — Definitions

### 4.a Unable to Pay

A person is **unable to pay** if:

1. their household income is below **two-thirds** of the national median household income, adjusted for household size; or
2. paying the cost in question would deprive them of the means of subsistence.

A person's own sworn statement of their income and means is sufficient, unless the court finds specific reason to doubt it.

### 4.b Covered Proceeding

**Covered proceeding** means a civil proceeding in which a person may lose:

1. their home, by eviction, foreclosure, or any other order to leave it;
2. custody of their child, or contact with them, including a removal of a child by a government (Article I, Section I.1.j);
3. their liberty, in any custodial category that is not criminal (Article II, Section 2); or
4. their right to remain in Concordia, in a removal proceeding.

## Section 5 — Fees

No court shall charge a fee for filing, service, transcripts, or any other step in a proceeding to a person unable to pay. A request for a waiver shall be decided within seven days, and the case shall go forward while it is pending.

## Section 6 — Counsel in Covered Proceedings

### 6.a Right to Counsel

A person unable to pay who is a party to a covered proceeding shall be provided counsel, at public expense, from the start of the proceeding through any appeal. The person may choose counsel from a list kept by the Office of Civil Legal Aid, and may decline counsel.

### 6.b Notice

Every notice that begins a covered proceeding shall tell the person, in plain words and in a language they understand (Article I, Section I.1.f), of the right to counsel and how to obtain it.

### 6.c No Proceeding Without Counsel

A covered proceeding against a person who has asked for counsel shall not go forward until counsel is provided, except to protect a person from imminent harm, in which case the matter shall be reviewed again once counsel is provided.

## Section 7 — Office of Civil Legal Aid

### 7.a Establishment

The **Office of Civil Legal Aid** is established as an independent office of the federal government. It shall provide counsel under Section 6 directly, or through contracts with nonprofit legal organizations and private lawyers, and shall provide legal advice to persons unable to pay in other civil matters as its funding allows.

### 7.b Independence

No official shall direct the Office or any lawyer it funds in the handling of a case, or restrict the claims or arguments a lawyer may make for a client, including claims against any government.

### 7.c Director

The Office shall be headed by a Director, nominated by the President and confirmed by a majority of the Senate, for a single term of seven years. The Director may be removed only for cause, after notice and a hearing.

### 7.d Funding

Congress shall appropriate funds sufficient to provide counsel in every covered proceeding in a federal court or tribunal. The federal government shall pay at least one-half of the cost of every state's and territory's program that meets the standards of Section 6.

## Section 8 — Agreements to Give Up the Courts

Under Article II, Section II.1.e, an agreement made before a dispute arises, by which a consumer or employee gives up the right to bring a claim in court, is not binding on them. A consumer or employee may still agree to arbitration after a dispute has arisen.

A business shall not refuse a good, service, or job because a person declines to agree in advance to give up the courts.

## Section 9 — Constitutional Construction

This Act shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution, and in favor of access to the courts.

Nothing in this Act limits the right to counsel of an accused person under Article II, Section II.4.a.

If an application of this Act conflicts with a constitutional right, the constitutional provision controls.

## Section 10 — Severability

If any provision of this Act or its application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional, the remaining provisions and applications shall remain in effect to the maximum extent permitted by the Constitution.

## Section 11 — Effective Date

This Act takes effect on the first day of the next fiscal year following its enactment, except that Section 6 takes effect two years later, so that the Office of Civil Legal Aid can be established.
